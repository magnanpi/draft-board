📖 Advisor & League Settings

⚙️ Adjust League Size

Changing the league size adjusts how aggressively the AI warns you about tier cliffs and positional scarcity.

🎯 Best Player Available (BPA) vs Need

The advisor constantly weighs raw consensus value against your starting roster holes (QB, RB1/RB2, WR1/WR2, TE, FLEX). If you are deficient at RB, it prioritizes the highest tier RB before talent falls off a cliff.

🔥 What is a "STEAL" (+10 ECR vs ADP)?

Calculated directly from expert rankings vs public draft ADP. If a player is marked +10 STEAL, experts evaluate them 10+ spots higher than the general public is drafting them. Jump on them before anyone notices!

⚠️ What is a "REACH" (-10 Reach)?

A Reach means the public is taking this player significantly ahead of where expert consensus ranks them. Unless they are the last guy in a critical positional tier, you are paying a heavy premium.

📅 Bye-Week Conflict Shield

In deep leagues, bye weeks can cost you matchups. The advisor checks your active starting slots and warns you if 3 or more starters share the same bye week.
